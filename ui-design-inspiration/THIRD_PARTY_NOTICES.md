## Corrections and removal

For an attribution correction or reference-removal request, [open a repository issue](https://github.com/Neel-Sh/ui-design-inspiration/issues) identifying the reference ID and original source. Do not post private information. The source index, manifest, and subsequent packages should be updated together when correcting or removing a reference.
