## S01 — Warm workflow panels

[Open reference image](images/01-warm-workflow.png)

**Visible:** three landscape-backed panels, warm desert light, translucent brown/olive rectangles, thin pale outlines, white connector lines, monospaced metadata, a prominent white approval button, completed task rows and a horizontal progress bar.

**Borrow:** visual separation of environment and controls; restrained workflow storytelling; a clear progression from connections to review to results. Its sharp geometry is an alternative to the large rounded cards elsewhere in the collection.

**Adapt:** the three-panel composition is a presentation, not proof of a real workflow or animation. Keep real status and actions explicit and provide a plain background when imagery reduces readability.
